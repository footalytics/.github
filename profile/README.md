## What Footalytics does

Ask about any upcoming match in plain language, for example *"Inter Miami's next MLS match"* or *"利雅得胜利 vs Neom SC"*, and get:

- ⚽ **Win / draw / loss probabilities**, with expected goals and the most likely scorelines
- 📊 **Context**: recent form, ratings built from real results, and key team news, with every source credited
- 🔁 **Post-match reviews** that compare the forecast with the actual result
- 📈 **A public accuracy dashboard** showing hit rates, Brier score and confidence intervals, including the misses

Leagues covered include the top European leagues, the Saudi Pro League, MLS, Liga MX, Brasileirão, the J1 League and more.

## Our principles

- **Transparent**: every forecast is recorded before kick-off and graded automatically afterwards. We don't delete results we don't like.
- **Honest about uncertainty**: football is low-scoring and random. A 45% favourite loses more often than it wins.
- **Privacy-first**: data is hosted in the EU, you can export your own data, and you can delete your account at any time.

## Responsible use

Footalytics is an analysis and entertainment tool for fans aged 18 and over. Its forecasts are probabilities, not guarantees, and they are not financial or any other advice. We don't offer, promote or link to betting of any kind.
